✨ Frequently Asked Questions ✨

How do I place an order? ▼

Simply click the WhatsApp button and send us a message with the perfume(s) you'd like to order. We'll confirm availability and arrange payment and delivery.

Do you offer delivery? ▼

Yes! We offer nationwide delivery across Botswana. Delivery fees vary by location. Local deliveries in Gaborone are often free for orders over P500.

What sizes are your perfumes? ▼

Most of our perfumes come in 30ml bottles, perfect for trying new scents or carrying in your bag. Our 555 Legend comes in a larger 50ml size.

How long do the fragrances last? ▼

Our perfumes are premium quality with excellent longevity. Most last 6-8 hours on skin and even longer on clothing. For best results, apply to pulse points.

Can I book a nail appointment online? ▼

Yes! Use our Nail Booking page to submit your request. We'll confirm your appointment via WhatsApp within 24 hours.

What payment methods do you accept? ▼

We accept Orange Money, MyZaka, bank transfer, and cash on delivery (for selected areas). Payment details are shared when you place your order.

Is the 20% discount still available? ▼

Yes! Our grand opening special gives you 20% off when you buy 2 or more perfumes. It's a limited-time offer for our first customers, so grab it while it lasts!
